Dear all,

We are delighted to invite you to request registration for the **Global Digital Collaboration Conference (GDC26)**, taking place in person at **Palexpo, Geneva, from 1 to 3 September 2026**.

GDC26 is one of the major international gatherings dedicated to the future of **digital identity, wallets, verifiable credentials, trust frameworks and interoperable digital infrastructure**.

🌍 The conference will bring together around **2,000 participants** from public authorities, international organisations, standardisation bodies, open-source communities, industry and research.

Hosted by the **Swiss Confederation**, GDC26 is co-organised by a broad coalition of leading organisations.

**Aptitude is participating as part of the EUDIW Group, together with WE BUILD**, alongside organisations such as **IATA, FIDO Alliance, W3C, ISO, OpenID Foundation** and many others.



### ✨ What to expect

The event will run over three days, combining high-level strategic exchanges with hands-on technical collaboration.

**🎤 Day 1 — Tuesday, 1 September**

Plenary sessions and global updates, with high-level speakers sharing strategic perspectives on digital identity, wallets and trusted infrastructure at global scale.

**🤝 Days 2 and 3 — Wednesday 2 and Thursday 3 September**

Collaborative workshops, technical deep-dives and interactive sessions on interoperability, standards, privacy, security, trust frameworks and sectoral use cases.

### 🇪🇺 Aptitude contributions

Aptitude will actively contribute to the programme, with sessions including:

- **Scaling digital identity in Europe: insights from Large-Scale Pilots**  
  *(Aptitude & WE BUILD)*

- **Enabling interoperable, privacy-preserving digital travel**

- **Building global trust in digital identity: the role of interoperable trust registries**

- **Digital Travel Credentials (DTC): standardisation status and next steps**

Additional contributions and workshops are expected across days 2 and 3.
