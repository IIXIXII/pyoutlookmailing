Dear consortium members,

On 20–21 April 2026, Work Package Leaders and SPOCs gathered in Berlin for Aptitude Connect, the first in-person consortium meeting since the launch of the project. We would like to share a short update on the key outcomes of this important milestone with the wider consortium.

These two days confirmed both the strong momentum and the growing maturity of Aptitude. Discussions highlighted substantial progress across all Work Packages, with solid foundations now established through initial analyses, governance frameworks, technical specifications, and use case definitions. The exchanges were constructive, well aligned, and strongly focused on collective delivery.

Aptitude Connect also marked a clear transition towards the implementation phase of the project. Building on this shared baseline, the coming months will focus on technical experimentation, pilot preparation, interoperability activities, and reinforced coordination across Work Packages and at national level.

The workshops and plenary sessions also reinforced the importance of maintaining close alignment between technical, regulatory, and operational dimensions, while ensuring that the project remains focused on concrete and meaningful use cases for citizens and service providers across Europe.

We would like to warmly thank DSGV for hosting the event, as well as all Work Package Leaders and SPOCs for their strong engagement and contributions throughout these two days in Berlin.

We look forward to continuing this momentum together with the entire consortium.
