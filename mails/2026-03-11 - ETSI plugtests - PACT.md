Dear all,

I would like to relay an invitation from ETSI that may be of interest to many actors involved in the **EUDI Wallet ecosystem**.

ETSI’s Centre for Testing and Interoperability, together with the ETSI Technical Committee for Electronic Signatures and Trust Infrastructures (**TC ESI**), is organising an **Electronic Attestation of Attributes (EAA) Plugtests™** event.  

The event will take place **remotely from 4 May to 1 June 2026** and is co-funded by the **European Union (EU)** and the **European Free Trade Association (EFTA)**.

The objective of this Plugtests campaign is to **verify interoperability of Electronic Attestations of Attributes (EAA)**, which are a key component of the **European Digital Identity Wallet (EUDI Wallet)**.  

The testing will focus on **ETSI TS 119 472-1**, with particular attention to formats based on **SD-JWT VC** and **ISO 18013-5**.

Testing will be conducted via a **dedicated web portal available 24/7**, allowing participants to perform tests at their own pace. The campaign will include:

- generation and cross-verification tests  
- verification-only tests (negative tests)  
- EAA conformance tests  

Developers, providers and stakeholders working on **EAA or EUDI Wallet solutions** are encouraged to participate.

Participation is **free of charge** and open to **ETSI members as well as non-members**.
