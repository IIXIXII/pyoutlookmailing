Dear UNFOLD Community,

We are pleased to invite you to **EUDIW UNFOLD #5**, taking place on **7–8 October 2026 in Nijmegen, the Netherlands**.

For its fifth edition, UNFOLD is moving from Paris to the Netherlands, bringing together public-sector and business leaders, service owners, consultants, technology providers and digital identity experts at **Pathé Nijmegen**.

As the European Digital Identity Wallet ecosystem moves from strategy and experimentation towards deployment and operational readiness, **UNFOLD #5 will focus on one central objective: turning digital wallet ambitions into practical implementation**.

Through keynotes, real-world demonstrations, hands-on workshops and sector-focused sessions, participants will explore how digital identity wallets and verifiable credentials can be applied across:

- public services and local government;
- banking and insurance;
- education and healthcare;
- mobility and other business services.

The programme will address the practical dimensions of implementation, including **user journeys, interoperability, governance, integration and user adoption**. It will also provide a valuable opportunity to exchange experiences with Dutch and European stakeholders who are actively preparing for the deployment of the EUDI Wallet ecosystem.
