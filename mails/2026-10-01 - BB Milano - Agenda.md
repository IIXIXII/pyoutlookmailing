Dear all,

The 2nd APTITUDE Board of Beneficiaries Meeting is now only a few days away, and we are very much looking forward to welcoming you to Milan on 5–6 October 2026, hosted by Politecnico di Milano.

📅 Agenda

The latest version of the agenda is available here.

Over the two days, we will:

 - review the progress made by the consortium during its first year, with updates from each work package;
 - hear from key stakeholders across the ecosystem, including contributions from the European Commission and a roundtable discussion on use case adoption;
 - work together in dedicated workshops to prepare the next phase of pilot activities;
 - attend demonstrations showcasing progress within APTITUDE and the wider EUDI Wallet ecosystem.

🍸 Networking reception

Monday’s programme will conclude with a cocktail reception from 17:00.

This will provide an opportunity to continue the day’s discussions and reconnect with fellow consortium members in a more informal setting.

📢 Event communication

To facilitate communication during the meeting, we have created a dedicated WhatsApp group for practical announcements and real-time updates from the organising team.

The group is configured in announcement mode, meaning that only administrators can post messages, helping to keep the information clear and easy to follow.

👉 Join the WhatsApp group here

Should you be unable to use WhatsApp, please let us know. We will add you to a dedicated Slack channel where the same information will be shared.

📍 Practical information

The meeting will take place at:

        Politecnico di Milano – Leonardo Campus
        Via Edoardo Bonardi 3
        20133 Milan, Italy
        Metro: M2 line, Piola station

Registration and welcome coffee will be available from 09:00 on both days. We recommend arriving slightly in advance to collect your badge and settle in before the sessions begin.

As APTITUDE enters its second year, this meeting will be an important opportunity to align on our priorities, prepare the upcoming implementation phase and shape the next steps of the project together.

We wish you a safe journey to Milan and look forward to seeing you very soon.
