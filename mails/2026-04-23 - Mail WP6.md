Dear WP6 Members, dear Consortium Members,

I am writing to share an important update regarding the status of WP6, with implications for the broader consortium.

## 🔎 Current Status and Key Achievements

First, I would like to acknowledge the significant progress achieved over the past months. Thanks to the strong engagement of WP6 contributors, we have established a solid and credible technical foundation:

- **TS12 v1.0** has been formally published via NiScy/ARF, fully aligned with European Commission expectations;  
- An enhanced **TS12 v2.0** has been developed within APTITUDE, integrating early feedback from the ecosystem;  
- A **functional proof-of-concept** has been successfully implemented in the Paradym/Animo Wallet;  
- Dedicated sub-working groups (Design Rules, Fraud Signals, and specific topics such as offline, crypto and agentic payments) are now well structured with clear technical scopes.

These achievements position WP6 as a key building block for payment-related use cases within the EUDI Wallet framework.

## ⚖️ Strategic Dependencies and External Context

However, as we move towards piloting and potential deployment, several critical external dependencies require alignment at European level and impact the consortium as a whole:

- Ongoing discussions with the **European Payments Council (EPC)** regarding the acceptance of the EUDI Wallet for SCA;  
- Pending **formal positioning from the European Commission**, necessary to ensure full compliance with the political and regulatory framework;  
- The need to maintain **interoperability across Member States**, avoiding fragmentation in implementation choices;  
- Remaining **regulatory clarifications**, particularly on liability and outsourcing in payment use cases.

## ⏸️ Decision: Strategic Synchronization Phase

In this context, and in order to ensure both the relevance and the operational viability of our outputs, we have decided to initiate a **strategic synchronization phase**, implying a temporary pause of active WP6 development activities.

This decision is taken at consortium level and aims to safeguard the value of the work already delivered, while ensuring alignment with upcoming regulatory and policy decisions.

## ▶️ Next Steps

The next steps are as follows: 
- The Consortium Coordinator will engage with the European Commission to secure the necessary strategic clarifications and alignment;  
- Based on these outcomes, WP6 will be **reactivated with a clear mandate**, focused on pilot implementation and operational deployment;  
- The consortium will be kept regularly informed to ensure full transparency and coordination across work packages.

We remain fully available to discuss this evolution and to address any questions.

Thank you again for your commitment, the quality of your contributions, and your continued support.
