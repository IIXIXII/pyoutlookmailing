Dear all,

I am pleased to invite all members of Aptitude, as well as our European partners and ecosystem stakeholders, to join us in Moldova for an important event dedicated to digital transformation and digital identity.

📅 The Moldova Digital Summit 2026, taking place on 5–6 June 2026 in Chișinău, is the leading digital innovation and policy event in Moldova and one of the key digital gatherings in Eastern Europe.

The summit will bring together government representatives, European institutions, technology companies, startups, investors, and digital identity experts to discuss major topics such as:
- digital public services;
- interoperability;
- artificial intelligence;
- cybersecurity;
- digital wallets;
- and European digital integration.

🔹 Several dedicated sequences involving Aptitude and our partners will take place throughout the summit. In particular, we will organize:
- an Aptitude-focused session dedicated to the project and its strategic objectives;
- an interoperability workshop bringing together ecosystem stakeholders;
- and multiple exchanges related to digital identity, trust services, and the European Digital Identity Wallet ecosystem.

🤝 We believe this summit represents an excellent opportunity to strengthen cooperation between European stakeholders, engage with emerging digital ecosystems, and contribute to the ongoing discussions around trusted and interoperable digital infrastructures across Europe.
