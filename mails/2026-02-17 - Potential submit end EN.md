Dear partners,

We would like to inform you that the **final report of the Potential project** was submitted to the European Commission yesterday evening. As you may have noticed, an automatic notification has been issued through the Funding & Tenders Portal.

The report is now under evaluation by the Commission. We are therefore entering an iterative assessment phase during which the Commission may request additional information, clarifications, or further justification on specific elements of the report.  

Should this occur, we may revert to some of you for complementary inputs. We would greatly appreciate your responsiveness in order to ensure a smooth and timely evaluation process.

Please also note that the **final payment of the EU contribution**, and its subsequent redistribution by the Coordinator, will only take place once the evaluation process has been completed and formally validated by the Commission.

As an indicative timeline, the Commission has up to **90 days** to proceed with the final payment to the consortium. This period may be suspended each time the report is returned to us for adjustments or additional information.

We would like to warmly thank you for your cooperation and commitment throughout the preparation of this final report.  

We remain at your disposal should you have any questions.
