Dear Partners,

We would like to inform you that we have received the official evaluation outcome for the **EUDIW PACT** proposal under the **DIGITAL-2025-BESTUSE-TECH-09-WALLET** call.

Unfortunately, the proposal has not been selected for funding. It obtained an overall score of **9.5/15**, which is below the minimum threshold of **10/15**.

The decisive point in the evaluation relates to the **Relevance** criterion, which scored **2/5** and therefore did not reach the required threshold of **3/5**. According to the evaluation, the proposal was not considered sufficiently aligned with the objectives and expected outcomes of the call, in particular regarding the ambition and timeline for wallet certification.

The evaluators highlighted, in particular, that only one certified wallet was planned by the end of 2026 despite the large number of participating Member States, and that the proposal therefore did not sufficiently contribute to the objective of achieving certified wallets across Member States by the end of 2026. They also pointed to the insufficient consideration of public procurement activities in the context of European Business Wallets, the need for a more detailed approach to supporting the accreditation of Conformity Assessment Bodies, and a contribution to broader EU policy objectives considered too generic.

At the same time, the evaluation also recognised several important strengths of the proposal. The evaluators positively noted the maturity and readiness of the project, the value of developing a joint and modular certification scheme, the proposed cross-border certification approach, the capacity and expertise of the consortium, and the potential contribution to reducing fragmentation within the European digital identity ecosystem.

It is also important to underline that the proposal passed the thresholds for both **Implementation** and **Impact**, with scores of **3.5/5** and **4/5** respectively. While the final outcome is disappointing, these results confirm the strength of the network we have collectively built and the relevance of many of the activities proposed.

For transparency, please find attached the official rejection letter and the Evaluation Summary Report. No administrative action is required from partners.

On behalf of the coordination team, we would like to sincerely thank all partners for their commitment, expertise and contributions throughout the preparation of this ambitious proposal. Building such a broad and high-level European consortium within a demanding timeframe was already a significant collective achievement.

Looking ahead, the topics addressed by PACT remain highly relevant for the EUDI Wallet ecosystem: certification, conformity assessment, interoperability, cross-border recognition and practical deployment. We therefore propose to explore whether there is interest in maintaining the network beyond this specific funding opportunity through an informal **PACT Community**.

This community would allow interested organisations to continue exchanging knowledge, sharing developments, coordinating views where useful, and identifying future cooperation opportunities.
