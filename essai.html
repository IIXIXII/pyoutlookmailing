Dear all,

As we approach the first anniversary of the Aptitude consortium, I am delighted to invite you to our second Board of Beneficiaries meeting, which will take place in Milan on 5–6 October 2026.

We will be warmly hosted by Politecnico di Milano at its Leonardo Campus.

This meeting comes at an important stage in the life of our project. In particular, the meeting will provide an important opportunity to prepare the launch of the use case implementation phase and to ensure strong alignment across the consortium.

My sincere thanks go to Politecnico di Milano for hosting us, and to all partners for their continued commitment and collaboration in moving the project forward. Meeting in person remains essential to maintaining our collective momentum, addressing upcoming challenges and ensuring effective coordination across the consortium.
