🚇 **How to get there**

The closest metro station is **Piola, on the M2 line**, approximately a five-minute walk from the campus.

- **From Milan Linate Airport (LIN):** take the M4 metro to Dateo, followed by a 10–15-minute walk. The overall journey takes approximately 20–30 minutes.
- **From Milan Malpensa Airport (MXP):** take the Malpensa Express, then the M2 metro to Piola. The overall journey takes approximately 60 minutes.
- **From Bergamo Airport (BGY):** take an airport shuttle, then the M2 metro to Piola. The overall journey takes approximately 70–80 minutes.
- **From Milan city centre:** take the M2 metro to Piola. The journey takes approximately 15–20 minutes.
