Dear WP6 Members, dear Consortium Members,

I am pleased to share an important update regarding the status of WP6 and the next phase of our work.

## 🔎 Background

As you may recall, earlier this year we initiated a **strategic synchronization phase** to safeguard the value of the work already delivered while aligning with the European Commission and the broader ecosystem on several key dependencies:

- 🏦 Acceptance of the EUDI Wallet for Strong Customer Authentication (SCA) within the European payments ecosystem;
- 🇪🇺 Formal positioning from the European Commission;
- 🔗 Convergence towards interoperable implementations across Member States;
- 📜 Regulatory clarifications regarding liability and outsourcing in payment scenarios.

This pause allowed us to ensure that the future evolution of WP6 would remain fully aligned with the direction of the European Digital Identity ecosystem.

## ✅ Progress Achieved During the Synchronization Phase

Over the past months, significant work has continued behind the scenes to prepare for a robust and sustainable restart.

Among the main achievements:

- 🤝 Several strategic exchanges took place with the **European Commission**, including discussions clarifying the scope and application of Strong Customer Authentication;
- 💬 Several bilateral meetings between **DSGV** and **France Titres** resulted in a proposal to define implementation profiles, allowing each wallet to select a compatible configuration rather than a single rigid model;
- 🧩 Building on this work, a **Common Core + Modular Framework** architecture has been proposed and discussed as the evolution of **TS12 within PASO**, explicitly designed to accommodate the diversity of wallet strategies across the consortium, including government-issued wallets (documents-first) and private/bank-issued wallets (payments-first), while preserving a common interoperable foundation;
- 🎯 This architecture, together with a live demonstration of **issuer-controlled dynamic transaction metadata**, was presented during a dedicated workshop and received very positive feedback.

These developments provide the strategic foundation that was missing when WP6 entered the synchronization phase.

## ▶️ Decision: Reactivation of WP6

Based on this progress, I am pleased to confirm that **WP6 is officially resuming its regular technical activities**.

Weekly meetings will resume initially, with the objective of:

- 📖 Consolidating the PASO specification together with all WP6 participants;
- 🧪 Preparing interoperable pilot implementations;
- 🚀 Moving from architectural design towards operational deployment.

This marks the beginning of a new phase for WP6, building on the excellent work already accomplished by the consortium.

## 🛠️ Next Steps

During the coming weeks, we will focus on the following activities:

- 📅 Updated meeting invitations and an updated participant list will shortly be circulated to all WP6 members;
- 📘 The **PASO specification** will be circulated in parallel for full review by all WP6 participants;
- ⚙️ Building on the validated **Common Core**, work will now focus on implementation by refining the different implementation profiles across wallet types (government-issued and private/bank-issued) and testing them rapidly to confirm they hold up in practice;
- 🧪 Integration scenarios will be consolidated across participants to clarify who is implementing which profile, particularly for payment use cases, allowing interoperability testing to start as early as possible.

## 🤝 Looking Ahead

The synchronization phase has enabled us to transform an already solid technical specification into a more flexible and future-proof architecture capable of supporting the diversity of the European wallet ecosystem.

I would like to sincerely thank everyone for your patience, your continued engagement, and the outstanding quality of the work delivered throughout this period.

I am looking forward to working with all of you as we enter this new implementation phase.
